Stracciatella (Italian Wedding Soup)

Stracciatella (Italian Wedding Soup)

An Italian soup consisting of meat broth and small shreds of an egg-based mixture, prepared by drizzling the mixture into boiling broth and stirring.

Ingredients

  • 3 1/2 c Chicken broth
  • 1 lb Fresh spinach; wash/trim/chop
  • 1 Egg
  • 1 c Grated parmesan cheese (or) 1 c Romano cheese; freshly grated
  • Salt and pepper; to taste

Servings

4 servings

Instructions

  1. Bring 1 cup of the broth to a boil
  2. Add spinach and cook until softened but still bright green.
  3. Remove spinach with a slotted spoon and set aside.
  4. Add remaining broth to pot.
  5. Bring to a boil. Meanwhile, beat egg lightly with a fork.
  6. Beat in 1/4 cup of cheese. When broth boils pour in egg mixture, stirring constantly for a few seconds until it cooks into "rags."
  7. Add reserved spinach, salt and pepper.
  8. Serve immediately, passing remaining cheese.

NOTES: Someone asked for this recipe a while back. I believe this soup, known as "Stracciatella" is synonymous with Italian Wedding Soup, however, I seem to remember from I-don't-know-where that Italian Wedding Soup is the same as this but with the addition of tiny meatballs.
